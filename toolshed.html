  • FixedHelp Guides Placement: Now keeps Prisma’s permanent Moe chat bubble above the bottom-right launcher, with both controls right-aligned, the existing edge and gap protection preserved, and reloads settling without repeated movement.
  • FixedSettings Toggles: No longer shows retired Loading Fact Review, Scheduled Chat Launcher, or Direct Moe Chat controls; the remaining Moe launcher placement adjustments stay active.
